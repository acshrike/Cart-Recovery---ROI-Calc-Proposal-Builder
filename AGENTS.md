# Rules for Cold Outreach

When generating proposal or outreach emails, YOU MUST adhere to the following rules:

1. Max 5 sentences for the first touch. Every word must earn its place.
2. Never pitch services in the first message. Pitch the problem + outcome.
3. One CTA only. Use "Interested?" or "Worth a call?" — not both.
4. Use their name once. More than that is considered desperate.
5. Do not use "I hope this finds you well." Ever.
6. Do not use phrases like "We offer a wide range of services." Instant delete.
7. Personalize the first line every time. Non-negotiable.
8. Do not reveal the price immediately. Reveal it only on the call after discussing needs.
